Hosting and local media

The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages, provided by GitHub, Inc. GitHub processes technical information, including visitors’ IP addresses, to deliver and secure the website. Our interest in providing a reliable website is the basis for this processing under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Images, videos and fonts are served with the website. We do not embed third-party video players or load Google Fonts.

Your feedback and Formspree

Submitting the form sends your selected interests, interest rating, optional comment and origin page to Formspree, Inc. Formspree delivers the answers to us and may process technical information such as your IP address and browser details for delivery and security. Processing may take place in the United States. See Formspree’s privacy policy.

We use these answers to evaluate interest in ITales and improve the concept, based on our legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. No email address is required. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in your comment. Feedback without an email is not necessarily anonymous to the service provider.

Optional launch notification

If you provide your email and select “Notify me when ITales launches”, we store your address and your request to send a launch notification. This is based on your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can withdraw this request by contacting us. The feedback form does not subscribe you to a recurring newsletter or send automated emails.

Optional Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited, only after you select “Allow analytics”. It measures pages visited, interest-button clicks, the start of form completion and confirmed feedback submissions, together with technical device and browser information. Analytics may use cookies and process data outside the European Economic Area. See Google’s privacy policy.

The basis for this optional processing is your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. We do not send your email, comments or individual survey answers to Analytics. Advertising personalization and Google Signals are disabled. Without your agreement, no Google Analytics scripts or requests are loaded.

You can change your choice at any time using “Analytics settings” in the footer. Declining stops further measurement and removes this site’s Analytics cookies. Withdrawal does not undo data already collected with your consent.

Storage and retention

Your Analytics choice is stored in this browser for up to six months, so we can respect it across pages. Analytics cookies for this site expire after six months. Event-level Analytics retention is configured to two months. Your answers are not saved in browser storage.

Feedback is held in Formspree and in the receiving email inbox for the duration of this interest study, and deleted when it is no longer needed. Notification email addresses are deleted after the requested announcement or withdrawal. Formspree’s free dashboard archive currently keeps submissions for 30 days; copies in our inbox are handled separately.

Your choices and rights

Contact us to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your personal data, or to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where applicable, you may request data portability. You can also complain to a competent data protection authority. For feedback submitted without identifying information, we may need details to locate the relevant submission.
