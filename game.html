01 / Game preview

Worlds to get lost in.

Follow scenes and chapters through atmospheric stories. Discover a world through its words, images and sound.

Every scene sets the mood.

Imagine arriving in a place you know nothing about. A few lines of story, an image and a shift in atmosphere can turn that first moment into the beginning of an adventure.

The ITales preview explores how scenes, chapters and optional audio could work together, giving you room to read at your own pace and discover a world step by step.

02 / Game preview

A choice changes the journey.

Take a different path, solve a challenge and see where your decisions lead. Each turn gives you another reason to explore.

Which path would you take?

A locked door. A stranger offering help. A clue that raises more questions than it answers. These are the kinds of moments where a choice could change how your story unfolds.

One path might lead to a new conversation, another to a hidden discovery. The idea is to make decisions feel like part of the adventure and invite you to explore other possibilities.

03 / Game preview

More than the next page.

Talk to characters, search for clues and collect items. Conversations and tasks bring you closer to the secrets of the world.

Look closer. Ask another question.

Sometimes the next step begins with a conversation. A character might share a useful detail, an item might reveal its purpose, or a small discovery might open a new direction.

These examples show the interactions we want to explore with ITales: moments that connect characters, clues and tasks to the story around them.
