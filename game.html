01 / Game preview

Worlds to get lost in.

Follow scenes and chapters through atmospheric stories. Discover a world through its words, images and sound.

Every scene sets the mood.

Imagine being part of a story that has something to say. A few lines of story, an image and a sound can turn that first moment into the beginning of an adventure.

02 / Game preview

A choice changes the journey.

Take a different path, solve a challenge and see where your decisions lead. Each turn gives you another reason to explore.

Which path would you take?

A locked door. A stranger offering help. A clue that raises more questions than it answers. These are the kinds of moments where a choice could change how your story unfolds.

One path might lead to a new conversation, another to a hidden discovery. The idea is to make decisions feel like part of the adventure and invite you to explore other possibilities.

Or imagine being faced with a difficult ethical or philosophical question that could influence the outcome of the entire story. These are the things this site strives for.

03 / Game preview

Interact with the story in different ways.

In addition to simple choices, there are various other ways to interact with the story.

Different types of interaction offer more variety and enhance the atmosphere.

In addition to making simple decisions, players can uncover secrets hidden in images, place items in the right spots to unlock new paths, find and run hidden files or programs on computers, engage in dialogues with other characters (which have fixed, predetermined outcomes), correctly combine terms, and much more.

04 / Game preview

Collect items, accomplish quests and get rewards.

Find items throughout the story to unlock new paths or get information. Accomplish quests to earn rewards or items.

Keep the goal in sight.

Try to complete quests that may appear at certain points in the story and earn rewards in the form of additional items or other rewards.
